======================================
  KAALU - your black puppy desktop buddy
======================================

KAALU TALKS BACK (new!)
  Kaalu now speaks his replies out loud (offline Windows TTS,
  puppy-pitched). Toggle in menu: "Kaalu's voice". Remembered.

VOICE COMMANDS
  Kaalu listens through your microphone (offline Windows speech,
  nothing leaves your PC). Just say, in a clear voice:
    "sit" - "speak" - "roll over" - "play dead" - "dance" - "howl"
    "fetch" - "good boy" - "jump" - "spin" - "come here" - "stay"
    "go to sleep" - "wake up" - "chase" - "shake" - "high five"
    "tell me a joke" - "stats" - "treat" - "hello"
  You can also prefix with his name: "Kaalu sit".
  A 🎙 bubble shows what he heard. Toggle in menu: "Voice ears".
  If he can't hear: Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone >
  let desktop apps access the microphone.

TRAINING KAALU
  Menu > "Give a treat" ....... +10 XP (~20s between treats)
  Menu > "Teach next trick" ... unlocks tricks in order:
    Shake 20 XP > Roll over 50 > Play dead 90 > Dance 140 > Howl 200 > Fetch 270
  He remembers training + settings forever (kaalu_memory.json).
  He only performs tricks he has LEARNED - even by voice!

HINDI MODE (new!)
  Kaalu ab Hindi mein baat karta hai (Hinglish)! Menu > Language
  toggle. Samajhta dono hai: baitho, bolo, naacho, palti, mar jao,
  haath do, koodo, so jao, utho, aaja, ruko, shabash, khana do,
  joke sunao, namaste, chrome kholo... Hindi jokes bhi!

APP LAUNCHER
  Kaalu opens ANY installed app (full Start Menu list, incl. Store
  apps): Menu > Launch > Any app (search), or type/say "open chrome".

EVERYTHING ELSE
  Volume/mute, brightness, system stats, app/website launcher,
  timers & reminders (he barks), lock screen, screenshot,
  empty Recycle Bin, Wi-Fi/IP info, typed commands (Talk to Kaalu).

CONTROLS
  Left/right-click .... command menu
  Drag ................ move him (drop him high and he falls!)
  Double-click ........ bark!
  Click while asleep .. wake him up

FILES
  Kaalu.vbs / Add-Remove Startup .bat / Stop Kaalu.bat
  kaalu.ps1 (his brain) / kaalu_memory.json (his memory)

NOTES
  - Brightness works on laptop screens; external monitors mostly block it.
  - Voice uses the default microphone; best in a quiet-ish room.
  - Only one Kaalu can run at a time.
